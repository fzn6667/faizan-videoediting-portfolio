00:01:12:04 — ABOUT

The editor behind the timeline

I'm a freelance video editor working with clients through Fiverr, cutting documentary-style videos, marketing and product content, and short-form social media pieces.

My work spans faceless documentary-style YouTube content, Instagram Reels and Shorts, and promotional videos for brands and products. I handle the full post-production process — assembling footage, structuring pacing, layering sound design and music, color, and motion graphics — so a script or a folder of raw clips comes out the other side as something watchable.

I'm comfortable working from a client-provided script and voiceover, from stock and AI-generated visuals, or from real footage shot for the project. Whatever the source, the goal is the same: clean cuts, a sense of rhythm, and a viewer who stays until the last frame.
